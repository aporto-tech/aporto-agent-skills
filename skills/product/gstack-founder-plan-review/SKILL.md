# Founder Plan Review

Use this skill when an agent needs to critique a product plan before execution.

## Process

1. Restate the user, pain, and desired outcome.
2. Challenge whether the plan solves a high-value problem.
3. Identify scope that should be expanded, reduced, or sequenced.
4. Surface taste decisions and founder-level tradeoffs.
5. End with a concrete execution direction.
